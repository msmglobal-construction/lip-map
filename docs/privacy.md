Lip Map (“the App”) is an entertainment joke-map for adults who use nicotine pouch products (“lip pillows”). It is **not** a quit-smoking or wellness product, and it is **not** intended for anyone under 18.

## Who this is for

The App is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. By using Lip Map you confirm you meet that age requirement. Nicotine products are addictive and not for minors.

## Information we collect

- **Location (When In Use only).** When you tap *Tucked*, the App reads your approximate location to drop a map pin. Location is not continuously tracked in the background. You can deny location permission; pinning will not work until you allow it.
- **Contacts (optional).** If you choose Invite from Contacts, the App uses the iOS contact picker so you can share an invite link. Contacts are not uploaded to our servers for advertising or sold.
- **Pins and app data you create.** Tuck pins, optional flavors, badge progress, and friend/league data you generate are stored on your device (and, when CloudKit or similar sync is enabled for friends features, in your Apple iCloud account associated with the App).
- **Purchases.** Subscriptions are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

## How we use information

We use the information above only to provide the entertainment features of the App:

- Drop and display your pouch pins on the map
- Show badges, dashboard joke-stats, and weekly unique-place leaderboards with friends
- Process and restore In-App Purchases

## What we do not do

- We do **not** sell your personal data.
- We do **not** use App Tracking Transparency / IDFA advertising tracking.
- We do **not** use HealthKit or collect health/medical data.
- We do **not** offer quit-coaching, nicotine milligram tracking, or wellness claims.

## Third parties

Apple provides App Store distribution, StoreKit billing, Maps/location frameworks, and (when enabled) iCloud/CloudKit sync. Their privacy practices apply to those services. See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/).

## Data retention & deletion

You can delete individual tucks in the App (History or Map). Uninstalling the App removes local data on that device. If you use iCloud sync for friends features, manage or delete related data through your Apple ID / iCloud settings as applicable.

## Children

Lip Map is not directed to children or anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the public repository [msmglobal-construction/lip-map](https://github.com/msmglobal-construction/lip-map) or contact the developer via the App Store listing support URL once published.
